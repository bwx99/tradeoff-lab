# Meridian Logistics — Facility Operations Handbook (excerpt)

This is a small, fully fictional policy handbook used only as reference
material for the Tradeoff Lab project. No real company data.

1. Employees may not work more than 10 consecutive hours without a
   mandatory 30-minute break.
2. Forklifts must be inspected at the start of every shift using the
   pre-operation checklist.
3. Any package damage must be reported within 2 hours of discovery.
4. The facility's on-time delivery target is 95%.
5. Overnight shipments must be sorted by 4:00 AM.
6. New hires complete a 3-day safety orientation before working on the
   floor.
7. Any injury, regardless of severity, must be reported to a supervisor
   within 1 hour.
8. Seasonal peak surge protocols activate when daily volume exceeds 120%
   of the rolling 30-day average.
9. Temperature-sensitive packages must be stored below 40°F and moved
   within 15 minutes of arrival.
10. Drivers must complete a vehicle safety check before every route.
